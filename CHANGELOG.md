## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-16

### Added

- Initial native macOS menu bar app for Vercel Analytics.
- Project discovery and switching for personal and team Vercel projects.
- Visitors, page views, trends, top pages, and referrers for 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day ranges.
- Local Keychain credential storage, private analytics caching, and disconnect cleanup.
- Developer ID build, notarization, and DMG verification workflow.
